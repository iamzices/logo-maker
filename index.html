Free logo and icon maker

EasyLogo is a free online logo maker built and maintained by Zices. Type a name to get a matching app icon from its initials and a wordmark from the whole name, in fonts like Pacifico, Fredoka and Nunito, with your own colors, gradients and 3D or sticker effects.

No login, no watermark

No login, no watermark and no upload. Every logo is drawn in your browser as real vector outlines, so the SVG, PNG and ICO files work anywhere, from favicons and app icons to websites and print.

EasyLogo is open source on GitHub. Find Zices on GitHub as @iamzices, or see more projects at zices.com.
