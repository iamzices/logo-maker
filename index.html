Free Logo Maker

EasyLogo is a free online logo maker built and maintained by Zices. Type the name of your brand, business, app or channel to get a matching icon from its initials and a text logo from the full name. No sign-up, no watermark and nothing to install.

How It Works

Type a name and both logos update as you go. Use one or two initials for the icon, set them in a rounded square, circle, blob or square, or leave the shape off, and try solid, inverse, white or black tiles to see what fits. Letters stay the same size from icon to icon, so a set of icons always matches.

Fonts, Colors and Effects

Choose from 13 open-source fonts, from cute, playful picks like Pacifico, Fredoka, Chewy and Bagel Fat One to Montserrat, Bebas Neue, Playfair Display and pixel-style Silkscreen. Pick a preset color or any hex code, then add a gradient, a 3D effect or a sticker outline.

Favicon and App Icon Generator

Download the icon as a favicon.ico with 16, 32 and 48 pixel sizes built in, or as a 1024 pixel PNG or JPG for app icons, profile pictures and social media avatars. Switch the wordmark to a square canvas when a site asks for a square logo.

Transparent PNG and SVG Logos

PNGs keep a transparent background, or you can set it to dark, light or your brand color. The SVG is drawn from the font’s own vector outlines, so it scales to any size and opens in Figma, Canva or Illustrator without the font installed.

Logos you make here are yours to use for a business, app, YouTube channel, merch or print, with no credit or license fee. Every font is open source under the SIL Open Font License or Apache License, both of which allow commercial use, including in logos.
